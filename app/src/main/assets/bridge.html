Full speed and laptop control
Get the laptop helper

Double-click blazeit-pc.bat on this laptop (Windows may ask you to confirm once). It finds the phone by itself, asks you to allow it on the phone, and opens this page at http://localhost:8787. From there downloads use every connection, the clipboard works directly, and the phone's Control tab becomes this laptop's trackpad and keyboard. Nothing is installed; close its window to stop it.

For the most speed, join this laptop to the phone's own hotspot. Through a home router every byte crosses the air twice and shares the router's time, which roughly halves the rate at best.

Parallel connections

One connection rarely fills a Wi-Fi link: loss keeps shrinking its window, so it spends much of its time recovering rather than sending. Several connections stumble at different moments, so the total stays near capacity. Four is the usual sweet spot. On a USB cable one or two is already enough.

Measure the link

This moves bytes the phone generates and discards, so storage is taken out of the picture. Compare it against a real transfer: close to this number means the network is the ceiling, far below it means storage is.
