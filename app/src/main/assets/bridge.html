Laptop helper

Full speed, the direct link, and the phone as a trackpad

Get the laptop helper

Double-click blazeit-pc.bat (Windows may ask you to confirm once). It finds the phone, asks you to allow it there, and opens this page at http://localhost:8787. From then on downloads use every connection, the clipboard works directly, it joins the direct link when you start one, and the phone's Control tab drives this laptop. Nothing is installed; close its window to stop it.

USB-C cable

No radio at all

  1. Plug the phone into this laptop with a USB-C cable.
  2. On the phone: Settings → USB-C cable → Set up, and turn on USB tethering.
  3. BlazeIt's Home on the phone then shows the cable's address. Open that one here.

Parallel connections

Per file, both ways

One connection rarely fills a Wi-Fi link: every loss shrinks its window. Several stumble at different moments, so the total stays near the link's capacity. Four is the usual sweet spot; on a cable one or two is enough.

Measure the link

Network only, five seconds each way

The phone generates and discards the data, so storage is out of the picture. A real transfer close to this number is limited by the network; far below it, by storage.
